Biography

I am a visiting researcher at the Hausdorff Research Institute for Mathematics, University of Bonn. Before this, I was an Institute Postdoctoral Fellow at the Department of Mathematics, IIT Kharagpur.

My PhD research in geometric statistics was carried out at IIT Kharagpur under Dr. Buddhananda Banerjee. I am from Khalsi in North Bengal, West Bengal, India.

PhD thesis: A Geometric Prospective of Directional Data Analysis.

Research interests

  • Directional data and statistics on manifolds
  • Change-point analysis
  • Topological data analysis
  • Stochastic ordering
