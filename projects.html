Directional statistics · Sampling

Probability models and sampling on curved tori

Published & ongoing

Developing statistically and computationally useful models for angular observations when the underlying sample space is curved rather than Euclidean.

Research questions

How can samples be generated efficiently on circles, spheres and curved tori? How does the geometry of the surface change the probability model and the resulting inference?

Approach

Geometry-aware transformations, efficient acceptance–rejection schemes and exact uniform sampling, extended to projected-normal models on recursively curved high-dimensional tori.

Applications

Simulation and inference for multivariate angular data whose observations naturally live on products of circles rather than in ordinary Euclidean space.

Regression · Geometric loss

Regression for circular and toroidal responses

Published & under review

Building semi-parametric regression methods for relationships involving angles, where ordinary linear distance is not an appropriate measure of error.

Research questions

How can linear predictors be related to circular responses, or one pair of angles to another, without ignoring periodicity and intrinsic geometry?

Approach

Möbius transformations define flexible regression curves, while area- and geometry-based loss functions support estimation without requiring a fixed distribution for angular errors.

Applications

Bitcoin and Ethereum price extremes, surrogate markers of astigmatism, and wind–wave directions during cyclones Amphan and Biparjoy.

Change-point analysis · Nonparametric inference

Detecting structural changes in angular data

Active research

Designing distribution-free tools to identify when the mean direction or dependence structure of a directional time series changes.

Research questions

Can abrupt changes in bivariate angular data be detected without flattening the observations or imposing a restrictive parametric distribution?

Approach

Intrinsic torus geometry motivates curved dispersion and angular covariance measures, leading to nonparametric tests for toroidal and spherical mean-direction changes.

Applications

Hourly wind–wave directions, cyclone tracks, and the angular representation of cryptocurrency timestamps at extreme values.

Statistical learning · Data geometry

Geometry-adaptive clustering and topological data analysis

Ongoing

Extending geometry-aware statistics to high-dimensional biological data, where periodic structure and topological shape can reveal patterns that Euclidean methods miss.

Current directions

Geometry-adaptive clustering on high-dimensional tori, deep circular clustering for genomic and proteomic analysis, and tests for structural differences using persistent homology.

Approach

Curved-torus energy gives a position-dependent dissimilarity, while held-out geometry selection determines when curvature is useful at a chosen clustering resolution.

Application

Joint backbone and side-chain torsion data for proteins, alongside planned genomic and proteomic studies.
