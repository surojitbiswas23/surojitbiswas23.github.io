About me

I am a visiting researcher at the Hausdorff Research Institute for Mathematics, University of Bonn, Germany. Previously, I was an Institute Postdoctoral Fellow in the Department of Mathematics at IIT Kharagpur. My research lies at the intersection of geometric statistics and AI, with applications across diverse fields.

I completed my PhD in geometric statistics at IIT Kharagpur under the supervision of Dr. Buddhananda Banerjee. I am from Khalsi, West Bengal, India.
